Our Journey

The Sahiwal Tech Community Story

In June 2023, Kashif Sohail, an AI Data Engineer, had a vision: to create a platform where tech professionals in Sahiwal could connect, share knowledge, and grow together. What started as a small WhatsApp group has now grown into a thriving community of nearly 1,000 members across three dedicated groups.

The community was founded on a simple yet powerful principle: "Share knowledge, opportunities, and guidance with those who need it." We believe that everyone deserves access to quality tech education, job opportunities, and mentorship – regardless of their background.

Today, Sahiwal Tech Community stands as a testament to what can be achieved when professionals come together with a shared purpose. We've helped countless individuals find their first jobs, navigate career transitions, and grow their skills through peer support.

Growth Timeline

June 2023

Community Founded

Kashif Sohail creates the first WhatsApp group with a vision to connect Sahiwal's tech professionals.

August 2023

500 Members

Community reaches 500 members as word spreads about the valuable opportunities being shared.

January 2024

Second Group Launched

Due to overwhelming response, a second WhatsApp group is created to accommodate more members.

1,000 Members Milestone

The community hits 1,000 members across three groups, becoming Sahiwal's largest tech network.
