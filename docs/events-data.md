## 2. DevFest Pakistan 2024 - Sahiwal Edition
**Date:** Saturday, 21st December 2024  
**Time:** 10:00 AM - 04:00 PM  
**Location:** E-Library Sahiwal  

### About the Event
After a successful online conference & Abbottabad event, GDG Live Pakistan & Sahiwal Tech Community brought the magic of DevFest to Sahiwal for the very first time! This landmark event was crafted to celebrate and empower the city’s vibrant and growing tech community. 

The one-day festival featured:
- ✨ Inspiring talks by industry experts
- 🤝 Networking opportunities with local and national techies
- 💻 Hands-on workshops for skill development
- 🎁 Exciting swags and fun-filled activities

### Speakers and Sessions

#### 1. Eric Bhatti
**Role:** Google Developer Expert (Firebase)
**Session Name:** Rapid API with Cloud Functions (Workshop)
**Description:** A hands-on workshop focused on building and deploying APIs quickly using Cloud Functions. Perfect for leveling up backend skills.
**Image Reference:** `assets/images/events/devfest-sahiwal/speaker-eric-bhatti-rapid-api.jpg`

#### 2. Kashif Sohail
**Role:** Senior Data Platform Engineer
**Session Name:** Your First Step Into Data Platforms
**Description:** Covered the essentials of data ingestion, transformation, and storage with real-world examples and practical tools like Snowflake, BigQuery, Airflow, and Terraform.
**Image Reference:** `assets/images/events/devfest-sahiwal/speaker-kashif-sohail-data-platforms.jpg`

#### 3. Farhan Ashraf
**Role:** DevSecOps Engineer
**Session Name:** Generative AI in Cybersecurity
**Description:** Explored how Generative AI is transforming cybersecurity through automating threat detection, driving proactive security in cloud-native environments, and ensuring ethical AI practices.
**Image Reference:** `assets/images/events/devfest-sahiwal/speaker-farhan-ashraf-security-ai.jpg`

#### 4. Abdul Raheem
**Role:** Data Scientist at RoyalCyberInc
**Session Name:** Why AI Agents Matter and How Gemini Makes It Simple
**Description:** Unpacked how Large Language Models (LLMs) enable AI agents to reason, plan, and execute tasks autonomously, and how Gemini simplifies building these intelligent agents.
**Image Reference:** `assets/images/events/devfest-sahiwal/speaker-abdul-raheem-ai-agents.jpg`
